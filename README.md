### 🚀 What I Build & Why

I focus on systems that don't just work today — they evolve, recover gracefully, and keep humans meaningfully in control.

- **Agentic & Autonomous Systems** — goal-directed agents with persistent memory, tool orchestration, reflection loops, and safety boundaries  
- **Real-time & Event-driven Backends** — high-throughput APIs, webhooks, WebSockets, and reactive pipelines that stay responsive under load  
- **Production AI/ML Integration** — inference at scale, drift detection, fallback mechanisms, and observability that turns models into dependable system components  
- **Composable Architecture** — middleware layers, clean adapters, explicit contracts, and modular designs that scale with team size and complexity  
- **Operator-first Interfaces** — dashboards and control planes that make complex, agent-powered systems understandable and steerable  

I care deeply about **leverage**, **resilience**, and **transparency** — building today while designing for 10× tomorrow.
